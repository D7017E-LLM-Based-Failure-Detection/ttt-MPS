Documentation

The MPS CD has a manual, circuit diagrams, a GRAFCET sequence and a video for each station type used here: 01_Distributing, 02_Testing, 03_Processing, 04_Handling (used twice) and 09_Sorting. The conveyor loop, its stoppers and the trays are not on the CD. The knowledge graph in docs/mps400/ records these facts in YAML.
